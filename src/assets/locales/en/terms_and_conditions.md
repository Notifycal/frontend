## 3. Service Description

### 3.1 Main Functionalities

3.1.1 The service comprises the following technical functionalities:

a) **Calendar integration**: We connect with your digital calendars through secure authentication, automatically accessing scheduled events without requiring manual intervention.

b) **Automatic recipient detection**: The system identifies and automatically extracts phone numbers from your calendar events, either from attendee contact information or from event descriptions.

c) **Flexible temporal scheduling**: Customizable configuration of advance times for reminder sending, allowing you to set different intervals according to your operational needs.

d) **Automatic processing**: Once configured, the system functions autonomously, continuously analyzing your calendars and scheduling sends without requiring manual intervention.

### 3.2 Complete Service Reminders

3.2.1 Users with active subscription access reminders that operate under the following conditions:

a) **Unlimited quantity**: There is no limit on the number of reminders you can send. The system will automatically process all events in your integrated calendars according to your configuration.

b) **Automatic sending per recipient**: One reminder will be sent for each unique phone number found in each calendar event. If an event has multiple attendees with different numbers, one reminder will be sent to each except the event creator.

c) **Continuous processing**: The system continuously analyzes your calendars and automatically schedules reminders according to your configured send time preferences.

d) **Geographic coverage**: The communication sending service is limited exclusively to Spanish phone numbers (country code +34). This limitation may be reviewed and extended according to Notifycal's criteria and commercial strategy.

e) **Temporal automation**: Reminders are sent automatically according to the schedule established in the calendar, which may include sends on holidays or at different times depending on the nature of each appointment. Send times depend on the temporal configuration of each scheduled event and the preferences you have established in your account configuration.

3.2.2 User responsibilities:

b) **Professional content**: All reminders must maintain appropriate and professional content, related exclusively to appointments, reservations, or services actually scheduled with recipients who maintain a commercial relationship with you.
